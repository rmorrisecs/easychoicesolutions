Getting started with continuous improvement

Continuous improvement does not require a massive overhaul. Start by noticing one friction point in your daily workflow, make a small change, and measure whether it helps.

Over time, those incremental wins compound into clearer processes, happier teams, and better results for your customers—that is the heart of Kaizen.
